// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Select a chain and network on this page, enter your wallet address or connect a wallet, then complete the verification to receive testnet tokens. The base drip is free and requires no Quicknode account or post on X. We do require a small mainnet balance on the wallet you are using to claim. This is to prevent abuse and spam. Optional bonuses increase the amount, but are never required.

You can claim once every 12 hours per network, and the exact base amount is shown on the next step, once you have entered a wallet address. Sharing a post on X that contains your wallet address and this faucet URL doubles the drip, and Quicknode customers on a paid plan double it again, up to four times the base amount plus priority in the queue. The cooldown is per wallet address per network, so you can still claim on a different network in the meantime.

A testnet faucet is a service that sends free test tokens to any address that asks for them. Developers use those tokens to pay gas while deploying contracts, sending test transactions and debugging failures, without spending real money. Testnet tokens exist only on test networks and have no monetary value, which is what makes it safe to hand them out for free.

The faucet currently supports 23 blockchains and their testnets: Ethereum, Polygon, Binance Smart Chain, Optimism, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Solana, Base, Blast, zkSync, Berachain, Morph, Cyber, Mantle, Story, Monad, Unichain, Ink, Plasma, Hyperliquid, Sei, Robinhood, Elysium. Use the chain selector on this page to pick a chain and see which of its networks are available.
